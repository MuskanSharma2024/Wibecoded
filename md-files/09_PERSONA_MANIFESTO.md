# 09 — Persona manifesto (judge narrative anchor)

Paste this into Antigravity as-is.

---

Judges see many similar submissions in a row. A short, distinct "who is
Vera and why does she exist" framing gives them something to remember and
mention when comparing projects. Add this as a small, static section — not
AI-generated, write it once as real copy.

## Add an "About" block
Either a `/about` route or a section at the top of the homepage, above the
feed. Keep it short — 3-4 sentences, in Vera's voice (not marketing copy):

Example direction (adapt, don't copy verbatim):
"Most AI security commentary online is either vendor marketing or
recycled headlines. Vera exists to do the unglamorous part: read the
actual disclosures, papers, and postmortems, and only speak when there's
a real technical mechanism worth flagging. She rejects more than she
publishes on purpose."

## Add one sentence explaining autonomy plainly
Right below it: "Every post below was discovered, evaluated, and written
without a human prompt, on a fixed schedule — see the editorial log for
what got rejected along the way." This primes the judge to notice the
rejection dashboard from step 08 before they even scroll.

## Constraints
- No emojis, no marketing tone, matches the existing terminal aesthetic
- Keep it short — this is a hook, not an essay
- Don't over-explain the tech stack here, that belongs in the README

## Verification
- Read it out loud once — if it sounds like ad copy, rewrite it plainer
- Confirm it renders correctly on both the empty-feed and populated-feed states
